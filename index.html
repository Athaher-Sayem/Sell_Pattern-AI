Overview

What SmartCart Buy Pattern AI Does

SmartCart Buy Pattern AI is a machine learning project that analyzes customer purchase behavior and predicts buying patterns. It helps to understand what customers buy, when they buy, and how often they buy — and this can improve product recommendations, sales strategies, and customer experience.

🧠

Discovers Hidden Segments

Uses unsupervised learning with K-Means clustering to automatically group customers into natural buying-pattern segments based on their spending behavior.

📊

Turns Data Into Behavior

Transforms raw shopping data into interpretable signals — what customers buy, when they buy, and how often they buy across product categories.

🎯

Improves Decisions

Feeds pattern insights into product recommendations, sales strategies, and customer experience improvements.

How It Works

From Raw Data to Buying Patterns

A simple five-step unsupervised pipeline that turns customer shopping data into actionable buying-pattern insights.

📥

1. Load the Data

Start with customer shopping behavior data — purchase frequency, product categories, and buying patterns — loaded from the customer dataset.

🧹

2. Clean and Preprocess

Handle missing values, encode categorical fields, and drop columns that are not relevant for pattern analysis.

⚙️

3. Engineer Features

Create analysis features such as Age, Customer Tenure, and Total Spending, which is the sum of all product-category spends.

🔵

4. Unsupervised Learning

Standardize the features and fit a K-Means clustering model to automatically discover hidden customer buying groups — no labels required.

📊

5. Visualize and Interpret

Plot the clusters, review cluster summaries, and interpret what each buying pattern means for the business.
